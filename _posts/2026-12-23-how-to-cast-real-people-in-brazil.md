Not long ago we were producing a music video for an actor who lives in France but comes from Brazil. The video was about reconnecting with where he's from, so he needed a real, typical Brazilian family on screen, not a cast that looked like one. Ten people, all ages, the kind of family you'd actually meet here.

We found them really quickly. That's the part about casting in Brazil that surprises people: when you have the access, real people are often easier to find than models.

## Why cast real people at all

For a raw, documentary feel, nothing beats it. Faces that weren't trained for the camera bring something a model can't fake, and audiences feel the difference.

We cast non-models all the time: influencers, locals, families, people of every age. Recent requests include that family of ten, a 65-year-old woman for another shoot, and pregnant women and mothers with toddlers for Narah Baptista's social project. For [Nike × NOCTA]({{ '/work/nike-nocta/' | relative_url }}), the whole cast came straight from the favela, with no agencies and no model cards.

## Three ways we cast

It depends on what you need.

### An open call

When we want to see a lot of faces, we run an open street casting. We spread the word on social media and through our network, which is big, and rent a space where we can take photos and ask people to try things out. We've run open calls over two days, and it's a really effective way to find people you'd never get from an agency.

### A specific profile

Often a client describes exactly who they need, and we already know who that person is. That's where years of relationships in the city pay off, and it's how a cast can come together in days rather than weeks.

### Agencies

For models at the top of their careers, we go through agencies.

## How real people get paid

Non-models are paid through us. We invoice it like any other cost on the production, and the people on camera get a normal, proper payment.

## Don't skip the paperwork

Real people need image releases just like models do, with the usage period, media and territory spelled out. In Brazil that matters more than in most places, and we broke it down in [Image Rights in Brazil]({{ '/blog/image-rights-brazil-model-release/' | relative_url }}).

## Our take

If you want your campaign to feel real, cast real people. Tell us who you're picturing, even if it's just "a grandmother who's lived in the same street for fifty years", and we'll go and find her.
