People ask us when to come as if there's a wrong answer. In Rio there mostly isn't. But there is a best stretch of the year, and it's starting right now.

## November to June: the main season

Roughly from November through May or June, Rio is warm and sunny and the days are long. It's the Rio everyone pictures: busy beaches, people out until late, skin and color everywhere. If your brief says summer, this is when it actually looks like summer.

Inside that season there are two big moments, and then a calmer stretch.

### New Year's Eve

Réveillon in Rio is huge, and if you plan it right you can make amazing content around the celebrations.

### Carnival

This is the one we'd really build a campaign around. Nothing else in the world feels like it, it's incredibly vibey, and the range is enormous, from the parades at the Sapucaí to the street blocos. And the build-up counts too: the weeks before Carnival are full of rehearsals, parties and blocos already out in the streets.

For 2027, the dates to know are:

- **Street blocos** start on January 16, 2027.
- **Série Ouro parades** at the Sapucaí run February 5 and 6.
- **Grupo Especial parades**, the big ones, run February 7, 8 and 9.
- **Ash Wednesday** is February 10.
- **The Champions' Parade** is on February 13.

If you want to shoot Carnival, talk to us now rather than in January.

### April and May

After Carnival, the city calms down a little. The crowds thin out and the light is still amazing, which makes this a great window if you want Rio's look without fighting for space on the sand.

## May to October: cooler, not closed

Rio doesn't really have a rainy season. From around May to October it's a bit cooler and a bit rainier, and that's about it. Plenty of shoots work well in these months, and our earlier guide on [shooting in Brazil]({{ '/blog/brazil-is-having-a-moment/' | relative_url }}) covers how the seasons play out across the rest of the country.

## When the light is good

Sunrise in Rio falls somewhere between 5 and 7 in the morning, a bit earlier in summer. In the afternoon, the light starts turning golden from about 3pm in winter and around 4:35pm in summer. We plan shoot days around those windows, so if you want a sunrise on the beach before the city wakes up, it's an early call time.

## Our take

If you can choose, come between November and June. If you want something nobody else has, plan around Carnival. And if you'd rather have space and calm, April and May are underrated.

Whatever month you pick, permits take time, so leave at least two weeks. The practical side is all in our [Filming in Brazil FAQ]({{ '/filming-in-brazil/' | relative_url }}).
