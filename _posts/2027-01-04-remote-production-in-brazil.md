You can produce a campaign in Rio without anyone from your team getting on a plane. [Soli&Sun]({{ '/work/soli-sun/' | relative_url }}) did it from Europe. LOX, Hidratei and Cantao shipped us their products and let us shoot them here. It works really well, as long as two things are right: how you approve things, and how you get products through customs.

## How approvals work from far away

We run remote shoots the same way we run any other, just with more on paper. You'll get:

- **Meetings** to align on the brief and the idea.
- **Presentations, videos and Canva decks** with casting and location options, so you can approve from wherever you are.
- **A production schedule**, so you know what's happening when.
- **A budget schedule**, so nothing about the money is a surprise.
- **A shot list**, so we agree in advance on exactly what we're capturing.

On the day, most clients don't watch live, but they stay in the loop. WhatsApp is how we normally work, we send updates as we go, and if you want a live video stream from set, that's no problem. You decide how involved you want to be.

## The lesson: customs

If there's one thing remote product shoots have taught us, it's to be careful with customs. When you ship products to Brazil, a lot can go wrong: packages sent back, stuck, or arriving with unexpected customs payments to settle.

So work it out with your production company before anything ships. Agree on the best way to send it, who pays any customs charges, and whether they can receive it with their local tax numbers, like a CPF or a company registration. It's a short conversation that can save the shoot.

## When remote makes sense

When the budget doesn't stretch to flying a team across the Atlantic. When the product can travel but your people can't. Or when you simply trust the crew on the ground and want a genuine Rio production without the logistics.
