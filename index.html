Why Work With Us.

We source and create narratives, not stock content. A few recent productions show how we work: real access, real locations, real Brazil energy.

CASE STUDY · BRAZIL PRODUCTION

Floyd

Floyd's campaigns are all about "bringing the mojo back". For this one, we set up a chessboard on Ipanema beach and shot the whole thing on 35mm film. Felt like it was pulled from another era, loved by audiences now.

CASE STUDY · TALENT SCOUT · LOCATION PRODUCTION

Nike × NOCTA

We helped produce Nike's NOCTA Cardinal Summer campaign in Rio. Cast straight from the favela, no agencies, no model cards. Shot inside people's homes, on the courts, in the streets at night.
