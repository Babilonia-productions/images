Case Studies

We source and create narratives, not stock content. Two recent productions show how we work: real access, real locations, real Rio.

CASE STUDY

Floyd

Floyd doesn't do generic campaigns. Every drop is a moment, the kind that stops the scroll because the story is different. For this one, we set up a chessboard on Ipanema beach and shot the whole thing on 35mm film. No digital clean lines, no studio lighting, just grain, texture, and real Rio in the background.

Editorial content that felt like it was pulled from another era, while it performed like it was made for right now.

CASE STUDY

Nike × NOCTA

We helped produce Nike's NOCTA Cardinal Summer campaign in Rio. Cast straight from the favela, no agencies, no model cards. Shot inside people's homes, on the courts, in the streets at night.

This is the kind of access you can't manufacture.
