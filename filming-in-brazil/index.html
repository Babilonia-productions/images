If it will also run in Brazil, we handle the registration, and because online and streaming placements are treated differently, we check the current rules against your media plan." - group: "ANCINE, Condecine and Paperwork" q: "Is Babilônia registered with ANCINE?" a: "Yes. The city of Rio requires a permit to film in any public location. You apply online, and processing takes 7 to 15 working days. We handle all of it." - group: "Permits and Image Rights" q: "Do we need image releases for talent?" a: "Yes, and in Brazil they have to be specific.
