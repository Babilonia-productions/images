## The Service
### Full Ambition. No Flights.

Remote production lets a brand shoot in Brazil without flying a team across the Atlantic. You direct from wherever you are, and our crew handles every moving piece on the ground: casting, locations, styling, crew and the shoot itself.

## Staying in the Loop
### As Involved as You Want to Be.

Most clients don't need to watch live, but they stay in touch with us throughout. WhatsApp is how we normally work, we send updates at every step, and if you want a live video stream from set, that's no problem. It's up to you how involved you want to be.

Some remote jobs are as simple as shipping us the product. LOX, Hidratei and Cantao, along with several smaller labels, have sent us their products to shoot in Rio, with no one from the brand on set.

## Proof
### One Bag. One City. Zero Flights.

[Soli&Sun]({{ '/work/soli-sun/' | relative_url }}), managing everything from Europe, needed its campaign shot in Rio without anyone getting on a plane. With Seasky Productions as executive producer, we took on the Brazil side: casting, locations, styling and crew, all in-house. Photography and video came out of a single production, executed to brief from thousands of kilometers away.
