## The Service
### Your Local Production Partner in Brazil.

If you arrive with your own director or creative team, we handle everything on the ground: crew, casting, locations and access, logistics, and the problems nobody planned for. We work in English and Portuguese, and most of our work is for brands and agencies from outside Brazil.

We regularly take on the Brazil side of bigger productions. On [DSQUARED2's SS26 campaign]({{ '/work/dsquared2/' | relative_url }}) we partnered with Mawe Productions on a night shoot in Rio's Babilônia favela. On [Nike × NOCTA]({{ '/work/nike-nocta/' | relative_url }}) we handled talent scouting and location production, with executive production by Eden.br. For [Soli&Sun]({{ '/work/soli-sun/' | relative_url }}), with Seasky Productions as executive producer, we ran casting, locations, styling and crew in-house.

## Who It's For
### Built for Brands Shooting From Abroad.

Most of our production-service clients come from Europe, and many from the United States. For a foreign brand, Brazil is hard to access alone: you have to comply with local rules, get access, speak the language and know the network, and you need a Brazilian production company on the job. That is the role we take on.

## Before You Land
### You Can't Just Turn Up and Shoot.

The most common mistake foreign crews make is assuming they can shoot anywhere. Locations need consent and the right legal clearances, and Brazil is strict about image rights, so the people who appear on camera need to agree to it. We sort all of that out before the shoot, not on the day.

The other surprise is price. Producing in Brazil is still much cheaper than in Europe or the US, but often more than people expect. Complying with local rules has a cost, good gear is an investment anywhere in the world, and we don't underpay people: models and talent have their own professional rates, and we respect them.

We know the streets, the people, who to ask for access, and how long the move between two locations really takes. That is the difference between a smooth shoot day and a lost one. It works at every scale, from a full international crew to a single photographer who needs a day of help getting around Rio.
