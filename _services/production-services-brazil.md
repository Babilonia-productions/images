## The Service
### Your Local Production Partner in Brazil.

If you arrive with your own director or creative team, we handle everything on the ground: crew, casting, locations and access, logistics, and the problems nobody planned for. We work in English and Portuguese, and most of our work is for brands and agencies from outside Brazil.

We regularly take on the Brazil side of bigger productions. On [DSQUARED2's SS26 campaign]({{ '/work/dsquared2/' | relative_url }}) we partnered with Mawe Productions on a night shoot in Rio's Babilônia favela. On [Nike × NOCTA]({{ '/work/nike-nocta/' | relative_url }}) we handled talent scouting and location production, with executive production by Eden.br. For [Soli&Sun]({{ '/work/soli-sun/' | relative_url }}), with Seasky Productions as executive producer, we ran casting, locations, styling and crew in-house.

## Before You Land
### Know the Ground Before You Arrive.

We know the streets, the people, who to ask for access, and how long the move between two locations really takes. That is the difference between a smooth shoot day and a lost one.

Planning a shoot? Our guide on [how to shoot your next campaign in Brazil]({{ '/blog/brazil-is-having-a-moment/' | relative_url }}) covers seasons, locations and the basics of the production rules.
