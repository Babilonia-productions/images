## The Service
### Stills That Feel Lived In.

We produce campaign, lifestyle and product photography in Rio de Janeiro and across Brazil. We source the right photographer for your concept, or support yours, and handle casting, locations, styling and crew around the shoot.

Most of our productions deliver stills and motion together from one crew. For [Soli&Sun]({{ '/work/soli-sun/' | relative_url }}), photography and video coverage came out of a single production as one complete content package.

## On Location
### From the Seawall to the Soccer Court.

[Coolway's]({{ '/work/coolway/' | relative_url }}) footwear campaign moved from the Copacabana seawall to a neighborhood soccer court. [Casa Rosa]({{ '/work/casa-rosa/' | relative_url }}) set vintage silhouettes into the sand, rock and greenery of Praia Vermelha and Flamengo. In Rio, the location does half the work.

## Product and Still Life
### It's About the Feeling, Not the Product.

We produce product and still-life photography too: accessories for Burga, hair products for LOX, Hidratei and Cantao, sun protection, skincare and more. In the end, a product shot is about creating a feeling, so we place products in real Rio spaces like hotels and cafés, or in a studio when the brief calls for it. Right now we're producing a still-life series for photographer Anna Pihan at the [Santa Teresa Hotel](https://www.santateresahotelrio.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and Chez Gregorio. Studios also make sense when you need a lookbook and a lifestyle campaign at the same time.

## On Film
### Analog, Done Properly.

Our producer Helene is an analog photographer, and we're connected to the best film photographers in Rio. We shoot 16mm, 8mm and large format when the concept asks for it.
