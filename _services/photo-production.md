## The Service
### Stills That Feel Lived In.

We produce campaign, lifestyle and product photography in Rio de Janeiro and across Brazil. We source the right photographer for your concept, or support yours, and handle casting, locations, styling and crew around the shoot.

Most of our productions deliver stills and motion together from one crew. For [Soli&Sun]({{ '/work/soli-sun/' | relative_url }}), photography and video coverage came out of a single production as one complete content package.

## On Location
### From the Seawall to the Soccer Court.

[Coolway's]({{ '/work/coolway/' | relative_url }}) footwear campaign moved from the Copacabana seawall to a neighborhood soccer court. [Casa Rosa]({{ '/work/casa-rosa/' | relative_url }}) set vintage silhouettes into the sand, rock and greenery of Praia Vermelha and Flamengo. In Rio, the location does half the work.
