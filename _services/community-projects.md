## The Service
### Small, Local and Rooted in the Community.

We don't produce big events. What we do is help artists, brands and creators make something real happen in Rio's communities: a social project, a small show, your own get-together. These are projects where trust and access matter more than scale.

We also help with influencer marketing activities: visiting a place and doing something social, a small presentation or talk, a music or dance performance. We can host up to about 100 guests.

Our network in Rio runs deep. Fabio has lived in the city for 40 years and knows the people, the places and who to ask, which is what makes a community project work on the day and leaves a good impression after it.

## Example
### A Social Project, Cast From the Community.

For Narah Baptista's social project, we found and brought together pregnant women and mothers with toddlers from the community. That is the kind of job this is: small, personal, and only possible with the right relationships.

## On Record
### Make the Moment Last.

The team that produces your project can also capture it in stills and film, so the story keeps working after the day is over.
