Who You'll
Work With

We're a small, hands-on team, and we keep it fun on set without being any less serious about the result. We like new ideas and we don't copy other people's work, so we're the right fit if you want to tell a story that hasn't been told yet, not a version of one that has.

Fabio Ventura
Community, Locations & Talent

Fabio has lived in Rio for 40 years and knows everyone. He's a local influencer and the reason doors open for us, from hard-access locations to communities far from the postcard. His network of talent, models, videographers and crews is the one we cast and crew from.

Helene Doetsch
Producer & Director

Lucia Pilar Gonzalez
