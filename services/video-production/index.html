The Service

Films With Documentary Instincts.

We produce campaign films, music videos, fashion films, branded documentaries and social content in Rio de Janeiro and across Brazil. Our approach pairs documentary instincts with local access: real people, real places, and stories that go beyond the obvious. Our producer Helene comes from journalism and documentary filmmaking, and knows which stories actually work for a brand.

We shoot digitally, or on film when the concept calls for it. Floyd’s SS26 campaign put a chessboard on Ipanema beach and was shot entirely on 16mm. For Nike × NOCTA, the 16mm footage later became part of the campaign stills.

Direction

In-House When It Fits. The Best Team When It Doesn’t.

What we make most: music videos, documentaries and fashion films. Helene Doetsch directs in-house, as she did for LOX and ProStandard. On bigger productions we build the team around the job, and on a production the size of Nike’s, the brand’s own creative team of 15 to 20 people is part of it.

Camera, lighting and grip come from Rio’s rental houses, and aerials come through a drone operator we trust. In post, we work regularly with editors Richard Camilo, Jorge Araulli, Samuel Chuengue and Gabriel Camacho, and we partner with production companies like Dear Stranger Films.

Branded Documentary

Stories People Choose to Watch.

Audiences follow, buy from and share the brands whose work feels real. A documentary-led film earns that attention instead of interrupting for it. Our documentary Me Chamam de Nativo for Beautiful Destinations is currently on the international festival circuit. Read why brands are investing in branded documentaries.

Common Questions
Do you shoot on 16mm film?
Yes, when the concept calls for it. Floyd's SS26 campaign and Nike × NOCTA were both shot on 16mm.
Do you produce branded documentaries?
Yes. Documentary-led films are a core part of our work, built on real access to people and places in Rio and across Brazil.
Do you produce music videos?
Yes. A music video usually comes with stills and behind-the-scenes content from the same shoot.
Do you have in-house directors?
Yes. Helene Doetsch directs in-house, as on LOX and ProStandard. For other projects we bring in the best director and team for the job.
Can you supply drones and cinema cameras?
Yes, through partners. Aerials come from a drone operator we trust, and camera, lighting and grip from rental houses in Rio.
