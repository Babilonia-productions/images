The Service

The Location Is the Concept.

We scout and secure locations across Rio de Janeiro and all 26 Brazilian states, and handle the access and permits that come with them. We know the ground: the streets, the people, who to ask for access, and how long the move between two points really takes. That includes the hard-access spots and the communities far from the postcard, because our Fabio has lived in Rio for 40 years and knows who to call.

For DSQUARED2’s SS26 night shoot we scouted rooftops, staircases and corners of the Babilônia favela where the city lights stretch out below. Magali Pascal’s ALEGRIA collection moved through Ipanema, the Centro Histórico and Urca. Casa Rosa found the quiet side of Rio at Praia Vermelha and Flamengo.

What Clients Ask For

The Classics, and the Places Nobody Else Has.

The beaches come first, and Rio has plenty, from Ipanema to quiet, nature-led coves. Then the typical Brazil scenes: bars and restaurants with real character, monuments, viewpoints and favelas. The hidden ones are where we have the most fun, like the favela in Rio’s North Zone where a recent ProStandard shoot became a motorcycle journey with stunts.

How Hard Is It?

Most of Rio Is Easier Than You Think.

Sugarloaf and the beaches are straightforward. Christ the Redeemer is easy to reach but has its own process, with a filming license to apply for. Private homes take a little more time, depending on exactly what you need, and we have good connections to villas, residences and houses in the mountains, with or without pools, older or newer.

We deliver scouts however suits you: photos, video, a shared deck, or a live video call from the location.

Beyond Rio

A Country of Settings.

Beyond Rio’s center and South Zone, we have scouted and shot in Niterói, at Grumari and the beaches further along the coast, and in favelas such as Cidade de Deus and Tijuquinha. Outside the state, we have worked in São Paulo, Minas Gerais and Maranhão. Brazil’s scale is continental: the beaches of the Northeast, the Amazon rainforest, the cities of the South. See how to choose your location in Brazil, and our view on shooting in the favela.

Common Questions
Can you shoot in Rio's favelas?
Yes. We work in communities where we have real relationships, including the Babilônia favela, where we shot DSQUARED2's SS26 campaign at night.
Do you handle location permits?
Yes. Access and permits are part of the job, down to the last location permit.
Can you scout outside Rio de Janeiro?
Yes. We scout and secure locations across all 26 Brazilian states.
Can we film anywhere in public in Brazil?
Not freely. Locations need consent and the right clearances, and Brazil is strict about image rights for anyone who appears on camera. We handle both before the shoot.
Can we shoot at Christ the Redeemer?
Yes. It's easy to reach, but filming there has its own process and needs a license, which we apply for. Sugarloaf and the beaches are simpler.
Can you find private homes and villas?
Yes. We have good connections to villas, residences and mountain houses, with or without pools. They take a little more lead time than public locations.
How do you deliver location scouts?
As photos, video or a shared deck, or live on a video call from the location if you want to see it in real time.
How far ahead do you need for permits?
City permits for public locations in Rio take 7 to 15 working days, and beaches and natural parks go through the city's Environment Secretariat. Drone work needs federal clearances on top. Tell us early.
