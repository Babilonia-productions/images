Own your data, or rent someone else's architecture

Adam Liska on the real foundation of GTM execution
▶ Topline Spotlight — Adam Liska with Pavilion's Sam Jacobs

Fresh off a $20M Series A, Airspeed co-founder and CEO Adam Liska sat down with Pavilion's Sam Jacobs on Topline Spotlight to trace the full arc — from DeepMind to founder-led sales to the unglamorous work of turning a founder's instincts into a system a team can actually run.

Jacobs put a sharp question on the table: do you own your revenue data, or have you quietly let a CRM vendor design the architecture for you? Liska's answer was candid. The principle matters even before the infrastructure catches up — knowing how to access and reason over your own data is what makes data-driven decisions possible at all, whether a human or an AI system is making the call.

The sequencing he describes: data first, then playbooks, then hires — never the other way around. Capture everything from day one, build living playbooks on top, and let that same discipline extend into how the whole organization runs. It's the throughline connecting a Nashville lunch that became a six-figure deal to a Series A four years later.

DN Capital backs Airspeed's commercial brain vision
▶ Adam Liska with Thomas Rubens, managing partner at DN Capital

Adam sat down with Thomas Rubens, managing partner at DN Capital, on why DN invested and where the category is headed. After 25 years in software, Rubens points to a persistent problem: organizations rarely have clarity on why deals are won or lost, and sales, product, and leadership are often disconnected on what customers actually want.

His bet: go-to-market is shifting the way search shifted to AI — from systems of record to systems of action — and Airspeed is positioned to lead the commercial brain category over the next decade. The conversation also covered Q2 agentic workflows, the new forecasting module, and the New York expansion.
