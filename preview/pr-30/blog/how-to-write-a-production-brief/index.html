How to Write a Brief a Production Company Can Actually Quote

December 28, 2026 · By Helene Doetsch

You don’t need a 20-page deck to get a good quote. You need a handful of answers, and one of them is the number most people would rather not say out loud.

Here’s what we actually need, and how a simple idea turns into a production plan.

The basics

To quote a shoot, I need to know:

  • How many models, and what kind of people.
  • How long, meaning how many shoot days.
  • Which locations, even roughly: beach, city, favela, nature, studio.
  • Which objects you want in the shot, from the product itself to props.
  • Which crew you need, like stylists and makeup artists, or whether you’re bringing your own.
  • What you need delivered, and where it will run, because that shapes image rights and talent fees.

That’s it. Everything else we can work out together.

You bring the story, we’ll fill in the crew

You don’t need to know how a production is built. That’s our job. Pitch me the story and I’ll tell you what it takes.

Say the idea is a beautiful woman meeting a man on the beach. They’re both using a phone case, the phone case is the product, and a little love story unfolds. From that one line I already know you’ll ideally want a makeup artist, a stylist and a complete photography crew, plus a beach, two people with real chemistry and the right time of day for the light.

The creative team brings the vision. We turn it into the people, places and schedule that make it happen.

Tell us your budget

This is the one people avoid, and it’s the one that saves the most time. Everyone has a budget. Nobody arrives saying “whatever it costs”.

So tell us the number. If you don’t want to spend more than a certain amount, say so, and we’ll build the best version of the shoot inside it. That’s much easier for both sides than fishing for a quote, getting a price that doesn’t fit, and going back and forth.

If you’re not sure what’s realistic, we wrote up what a shoot in Brazil actually costs.

What happens next

Once we have your brief, we get on a short call, then come back with a pitch and production plan: ideas, location and model options, and a budget that matches. We’re flexible on how we get there. If you’re still deciding between hiring us for the whole thing or just for the ground, this post explains the difference.

Send whatever you have, even if it’s half a page. A good brief can be short, as long as it answers the questions above.
