Do You Need a Permit to Film in Rio de Janeiro?

December 9, 2026 · By Helene Doetsch

Yes. The city of Rio requires a permit to film in any public location. The better news is that permits here are rarely hard to get. In our experience it’s never really about difficulty. It’s about time and cost.

The official rule

According to the Rio Film Commission, the city’s film office, filming in public locations needs authorization from the city. You apply online through a single form, and processing takes 7 to 15 working days. Beaches and natural parks are authorized by the city’s Environment Secretariat.

So if your shoot involves a public beach or a street, which most of ours do, build that timeline into your plan.

Favelas: the residents’ association

Shooting in a favela works differently. You need permission from the local residents’ association, and that’s a relationship, not a form. We help with that, and it’s one more reason never to film in a favela without a local who knows the people involved.

Museums, gardens and other institutions

Museums, the Jardim Botânico and other institutions each have their own process. We go to each one directly, because we know who to talk to, which saves a lot of back and forth.

It’s the same story outside Rio. In São Paulo, for example, Ibirapuera Park isn’t hard to get, it’s just expensive. That sums up permits in Brazil pretty well.

What it costs and how long it takes

A permit usually costs around R$1,000 (about $180), sometimes more and sometimes less depending on where. City permits take 7 to 15 working days, so for any shoot with public locations, give us at least two weeks, ideally more.

Drones are a separate process with federal clearances on top, and if you’re a foreign production, there’s also the ANCINE filing for the shoot itself. We explained how that works in Fixer, Production Service or Production Company.

Our take

Don’t try to skip permits to save time. Plan for them. Tell us your locations early, and we’ll tell you what each one needs, what it costs and how long it’ll take. All the paperwork questions are collected in our Filming in Brazil FAQ.
