The Service

Faces You Can’t Book Through an Agency.

We cast talent for campaigns, films and shoots in Rio de Janeiro and across Brazil, from agency models to real people found in the street, on the courts and in the community. Most of it runs through Fabio, who has lived in Rio for 40 years and whose network of talent, models and creators is the one we cast from.

For Nike × NOCTA, the casting happened straight from the favela: no agencies, no model cards, faces found through the community itself. For ProStandard’s NFL × Flamengo capsule, six faces from the real Rio were cast and on camera seven days after the first conversation.

How We Cast

Open Call, Known Faces or Top Agencies.

It depends on what you need. We have run open street castings, inviting everyone in over two days. When you need a specific profile, we often already know who that person is. And for models at the top of their careers, we work through agencies.

We cast non-models all the time, especially for a raw, documentary feel: influencers, locals, whoever the story needs. Recent requests include a family of ten for a music video, a 65-year-old woman, and pregnant women and mothers with toddlers for Narah Baptista’s social project.

Why Brazil

Range Is the Point.

Brazil has produced some of the most striking models in the world, and the variety of looks, styles and energy makes casting here both easy and exciting. Casting in communities takes care as well as access. Read our view on shooting in the favela.

Common Questions
Can you street cast in Rio's favelas?
Yes. For Nike × NOCTA we cast straight from the favela, with no agencies and no model cards. That kind of scouting only works with trust, and we come from the neighborhood.
Can you cast outside Rio de Janeiro?
Yes. We cast in Rio de Janeiro and nationwide.
How fast can you cast?
As fast as the project needs. ProStandard's six-person cast was found and shot within seven days of the first conversation.
Can you cast non-models?
Yes, often. Influencers, locals, families and people of every age. We recently cast a family of ten for a music video and a 65-year-old woman for another shoot.
Do you work with model agencies?
Yes, especially for models at the top of their careers. For other briefs we run open street castings or go straight to people we already know.
