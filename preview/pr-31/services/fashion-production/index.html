The Service

Fashion Campaigns With a Pulse.

Fashion is most of what we produce. We have shot SS26 campaigns for DSQUARED2, Ernest Leoty and Floyd, launched the ALEGRIA collection for Magali Pascal, and worked with labels including Farm, Arezzo and Lokoa The Label.

Rio gives a fashion campaign what a studio can’t: sun-bleached rock and tree cover, the Copacabana seawall, the rooftops and staircases of the favela at night. We find the place that carries the collection, then cast and crew around it.

Beyond campaigns, we produce lookbooks, e-commerce shoots and behind-the-scenes content.

The Team Around You

The Right Creative, Not the Usual One.

We have stylists, hair and makeup artists, photographers and videographers we work with regularly. But every creative sees the world in their own way, so we don’t default to the same names. We look at your style, what the project needs and your budget, and find the best fit.

Regulars include stylist Gabriel Menezes, hairstylist Igor Oliveira, and photographers Victor Pollock and Rafael Moura. On video, it is often our own Helene Doetsch behind the camera.

Why Rio

The City Does the Styling.

Most fashion clients ask for the beaches first, Ipanema above all, and if you want a quieter, nature-led beach we can find that too. Many also want the typical Brazil scenes: bars and restaurants with their own look, monuments, viewpoints and favelas.

We love going further. For a recent ProStandard shoot we took the production to a favela in Rio’s North Zone for a motorcycle journey, stunts included. DSQUARED2 came for heat and boldness and shot after dark in the Babilônia favela. Casa Rosa wanted something softer and found it at Praia Vermelha and Flamengo.

Common Questions
Do you produce fashion films as well as stills?
Yes. Most of our fashion productions deliver both from one crew. Floyd's SS26 campaign was shot entirely on 16mm film on Ipanema beach.
Which fashion brands have you worked with?
DSQUARED2, Nike × NOCTA, Farm, Arezzo, Ernest Leoty, Floyd, Magali Pascal, Casa Rosa and Lokoa The Label, among others.
When is the best season for a fashion shoot in Rio?
Rio works all year. December to March brings long summer days and peak energy, with higher prices. June to October is the Brazilian winter: pleasant weather, fewer tourists and more local availability.
Do you produce lookbooks and e-commerce shoots?
Yes, as well as campaigns, fashion films and behind-the-scenes content.
Do you have your own stylists and photographers?
We have regulars we trust, but we pick the creative for each project based on your style, what you need and your budget.
Can you book a studio?
Yes. We work with studios regularly, including on our Latin Grammys work. It makes sense when you need a lookbook and a lifestyle campaign at the same time.
