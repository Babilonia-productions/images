The Service

Your Local Production Partner in Brazil.

If you arrive with your own director or creative team, we handle everything on the ground: crew, casting, locations and access, logistics, and the problems nobody planned for. We work in English and Portuguese, and most of our work is for brands and agencies from outside Brazil.

We regularly take on the Brazil side of bigger productions. On DSQUARED2’s SS26 campaign we partnered with Mawe Productions on a night shoot in Rio’s Babilônia favela. On Nike × NOCTA we handled talent scouting and location production, with executive production by Eden.br. For Soli&Sun, with Seasky Productions as executive producer, we ran casting, locations, styling and crew in-house.

Who It’s For

Built for Brands Shooting From Abroad.

Most of our production-service clients come from Europe, and many from the United States. For a foreign brand, Brazil is hard to access alone: you have to comply with local rules, get access, speak the language and know the network, and ANCINE requires a Brazilian production company registered with it on the job. We are registered, and that is the role we take on.

Before You Land

You Can’t Just Turn Up and Shoot.

The most common mistake foreign crews make is assuming they can shoot anywhere. Locations need consent and the right legal clearances, and Brazil is strict about image rights, so the people who appear on camera need to agree to it. We sort all of that out before the shoot, not on the day.

The other surprise is price. Producing in Brazil is still much cheaper than in Europe or the US, but often more than people expect. Complying with local rules has a cost, good gear is an investment anywhere in the world, and we don’t underpay people: models and talent have their own professional rates, and we respect them.

We know the streets, the people, who to ask for access, and how long the move between two locations really takes. That is the difference between a smooth shoot day and a lost one. It works at every scale, from a full international crew to a single photographer who needs a day of help getting around Rio.

Planning a shoot? Our guide on how to shoot your next campaign in Brazil covers seasons, locations and the basics of the production rules. For permits, ANCINE, image rights and budgets, see Filming in Brazil: the production FAQ.

Common Questions
Can you act as our production service or fixer for a shoot in Brazil?
Yes. If you arrive with your own director or creative team, we handle the ground: crew, casting, locations and access, logistics, and the problems nobody planned for.
Do you partner with other production companies?
Yes. We have taken on the Brazil side of productions alongside Mawe Productions on DSQUARED2, Eden.br on Nike × NOCTA and Seasky Productions on Soli&Sun, and we partner with Dear Stranger Films.
Do you take on small jobs, like a day of fixing for a photographer?
Yes, when the timing works. Our focus is productions from $2,000 up, but we also help individual photographers and small teams with a day of fixing and production help around Rio.
Does your team work in English?
Yes. The team works in English and Portuguese, and most of our clients are brands and agencies outside Brazil.
What budget do we need?
There is no formal minimum. A full shoot day with a few models and both photo and video needs at least $2,000 to $3,000. A typical smaller production runs $10,000 to $20,000, and bigger, multi-day productions run higher.
Do we need a Brazilian production company to shoot in Brazil?
Yes. ANCINE, Brazil's national film agency, requires a foreign shoot to run under a Brazilian production company registered with it. We are registered and file the shoot with ANCINE for you.
What do foreign crews most often get wrong?
Thinking they can shoot anywhere. Locations need consent and legal clearances, and Brazil is strict about image rights for anyone who appears on camera.
Is producing in Brazil cheap?
Cheaper than Europe or the US, yes, but often more than people expect. Local rules have to be complied with, good gear costs money everywhere, and we pay crew, models and talent fair, professional rates.
Do you handle hotels, transport and catering?
Yes. We handle production transport and airport transfers, catering, and hotel bookings for a small fee. Translators are available if needed, though our team is multilingual, so it's rarely necessary.
