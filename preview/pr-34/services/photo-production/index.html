The Service

Stills That Feel Lived In.

We produce campaign, lifestyle and product photography in Rio de Janeiro and across Brazil. We source the right photographer for your concept, or support yours, and handle casting, locations, styling and crew around the shoot.

Most of our productions deliver stills and motion together from one crew. For Soli&Sun, photography and video coverage came out of a single production as one complete content package.

On Location

From the Seawall to the Soccer Court.

Coolway’s footwear campaign moved from the Copacabana seawall to a neighborhood soccer court. Casa Rosa set vintage silhouettes into the sand, rock and greenery of Praia Vermelha and Flamengo. In Rio, the location does half the work.

Product and Still Life

It’s About the Feeling, Not the Product.

We produce product and still-life photography too: accessories for Burga, hair products for LOX, Hidratei and Cantao, sun protection, skincare and more. In the end, a product shot is about creating a feeling, so we place products in real Rio spaces like hotels and cafés, or in a studio when the brief calls for it. Right now we’re producing a still-life series for photographer Anna Pihan at the Santa Teresa Hotel and Chez Gregorio. Studios also make sense when you need a lookbook and a lifestyle campaign at the same time.

On Film

Analog, Done Properly.

Our producer Helene is an analog photographer, and we’re connected to the best film photographers in Rio. We shoot 16mm, 8mm and large format when the concept asks for it.

Common Questions
Do you shoot stills as well as film?
Both, usually on the same production. Most of our campaigns deliver motion and stills together, from one crew, rather than two separate bookings.
Can you provide the photographer?
Yes. We have photographers we work with regularly, but every photographer sees the world differently, so we match one to your concept, style and budget. Or we support the photographer you bring with casting, locations and crew.
Where in Rio do you shoot?
Wherever the concept needs: beaches like Ipanema, Copacabana and Praia Vermelha, the Centro Histórico, Urca, neighborhood courts and Rio's favelas, plus locations across all 26 Brazilian states.
Do you produce product and still-life photography?
Yes. We have shot accessories for Burga, hair products for LOX, Hidratei and Cantao, sun protection and skincare, usually styled into real Rio spaces to create a feeling rather than a catalog shot.
Can you provide a studio?
Yes. We work with studios regularly, including on our Latin Grammys work, and often combine a studio lookbook with a lifestyle shoot on location.
Do you shoot analog?
Yes. Our producer Helene is an analog photographer, and we shoot 16mm, 8mm and large format.
