Rio Locations Beyond Ipanema: 12 Places We Love to Shoot

December 21, 2026 · By Helene Doetsch

Everyone asks for Ipanema first, and fair enough, it’s beautiful. But Rio is a lot more than one beach, and the shoots people remember usually happen somewhere they didn’t know to ask for.

These are twelve places we love, grouped by the kind of look they give you. Every public location in Rio needs a city permit, and favelas need the residents’ association on board too. We covered both in Do You Need a Permit to Film in Rio?

Wild beaches, close to nature

1. Grumari

Out in the West Zone, Grumari and the beaches around it feel like you’ve left the city completely. There are green hills, open sand and almost nothing built. It’s good for swimwear, outdoor and anything that needs nature without a long trip.

2. Joatinga

A small, hidden beach tucked between rocks. It’s gorgeous, but it comes with a catch: the tide. When it’s high, there’s barely any beach, so you need to know when to go. We plan the shoot day around the tide table, and that alone is a good reason to bring a local.

Rainforest and waterfalls

3. Tijuca Forest

A real rainforest inside the city, with waterfalls, trails and deep green light. It’s the place for anything that needs to feel lush, wet and alive, and it’s a short drive from the beaches.

Old Rio and city energy

4. Centro

Downtown Rio has colonial streets, grand old buildings and a mix of eras on every corner. For Magali Pascal, the historic center was one of the stops alongside Ipanema and Urca.

5. Lapa

Lapa is nightlife, music and old facades with a lot of character, and it gives you texture and grit by day or by night.

6. Flamengo and 7. Botafogo

City vibes, with water, mountains and everyday life all in one frame. Look out for the little food fairs, which are perfect for lifestyle and street-style content. Casa Rosa found a softer side of Rio around Flamengo and nearby Praia Vermelha.

Favelas

Rio’s favelas have some of the best views, colors and energy in the city. We only shoot in them with a local who knows the residents’ association and the people, and we wrote about doing it respectfully in Shooting in the Favela.

8. Babilônia

Our home turf, and the neighborhood in our name. Rooftops, staircases and views over Leme and Copacabana. DSQUARED2 shot its SS26 campaign here at night.

9. Cantagalo

Up the hill between Copacabana and Ipanema, with views over both.

10. Rocinha

Rio’s best-known favela, with views that are now all over social media, including the famous lookout at Porta do Céu.

11. Cidade de Deus

Out in the West Zone, with a completely different feel from the South Zone hillside favelas.

12. Tijuquinha

A smaller community we’ve shot in, and a good example of how much Rio has beyond the names everyone already knows.

How to choose

Start with the feeling, not the postcard. If the brief says “freedom”, look at Grumari. If it says “heat and edge”, look at the favelas at night. If it says “real life”, go to a food fair in Botafogo. Tell us the mood and we’ll send you options, as photos, video or live on a call from the spot.

And if you want the classics too, that’s easy. Ipanema, Copacabana and Sugarloaf are always there.
